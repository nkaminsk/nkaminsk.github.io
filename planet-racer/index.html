PLANET RACER

Lean, boost, and land it across five alien planets. Take on a 500-level campaign, push into an endless frontier, build your own racer, and compete in live four-player PvP.

Google Play: coming soon App Store: coming soon

Explore, build, and race

  • 500 campaign levels. Explore Dustbowl, Cryos, Verdance, Tantalus, and King's Rock, with 100 levels per planet, rival encounters, story beats, and a showdown every 25 levels.
  • An endless frontier. Continue beyond the campaign into generated constellations, with ten races in each and crown rewards to chase.
  • Your own garage. Unlock vehicle bodies, wheel sets, boosts, and paints. Collect cards and upgrade your collection from Common to Legendary.
  • Live racing for up to four players. Jump into a quick race or create and join public or private lobbies. Share a join code, use emotes, and compete in ranked arenas with separate PvP progression.
  • Rewards worth revealing. Earn reputation and season-pass rewards, collect daily crates, and reveal new cards and upgrades in full-screen loot animations.
  • Offline campaign, optional cloud saves. Play the campaign without a connection. Connect for PvP, purchases, or cloud backups; eligible players can link a Unity account to carry progress to another device.

Ads are your choice

Choose ad-supported rewards when you want them. PvP starts with ten ad-free races; each rewarded ad then adds twenty races, with room to bank up to one hundred. Daily-crate, life, and boost placements use short, skippable ads when available. A season pass replaces ad-supported rewards with claims and makes PvP ad-free.
