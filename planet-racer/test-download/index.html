How to install

  1. Open the testing link on your Android phone, signed in to the Google account invited to the test.
  2. Review the Google Play invitation and choose Accept invite to join.
  3. Follow the installation link Google Play provides. Use the same Google account in the Play Store.

During internal testing, Google Play may show the name us.illimite.spaceracer (unreviewed) instead of Planet Racer. That is the current test app.

Need access?

If Google Play says you are not eligible, email njkaminsky@illimite.us with the Google account email you use on your phone, so we can check your invitation.

Share feedback

Play a few races, try the garage and rewards, and tell us what feels good or gets in the way.

For a bug, include what happened, how to repeat it, and your phone model. A screenshot or short recording helps.
