They already use your products.
Make it easier to use them together.

Your products may have grown up separately. Your customers still expect to be recognized when they move between them. They shouldn't have to work out another entry point or wonder which account belongs to which organization.

InfiniAuth connects shared sign-in, existing accounts and product navigation. Customers choose their organization and continue with the account that belongs there. Each product keeps its own accounts and permissions.

Let customers connect their own SSO.

The standalone Keycloak extension gives a customer administrator a guided way to set up a SAML or OIDC connection.

Send a scoped link. The customer configures the connection, can test their sign-in and explicitly activates it. They don't need general access to your identity-provider administration.

Testing requirements follow the invitation's policy. The extension is separate from the InfiniAuth product layer.
